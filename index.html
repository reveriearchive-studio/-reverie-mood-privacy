Our Philosophy

Reverie Mood, a product of Reverie Archive, is a privacy-first mood tracking journal. We believe your mental health data should remain private and entirely under your control. Your emotional reflections are yours alone.

With the introduction of Letters to the Ether, you may choose to exchange letters with a close friend through the Shared Cove. This is the only feature that transmits data beyond your device, and it does so exclusively through Apple's iCloud infrastructure with end-to-end encryption for all letter content.

What We Store

On Your Device Only

  • Mood Entries — Your daily emotional states
  • Written Reflections — Text notes about your feelings
  • Voice Memos — Audio recordings (morning and evening)
  • Photos — Images you attach to mood entries
  • Doodles — Sketches and drawings from the doodle canvas
  • Grounding & Breathing Exercises — Wellness activity records and progress
  • Letters Desk Postcards — Personal postcards you compose, keep, release, or burn (never leave your device)
  • Companion Data — Your Puff companion's name and your display name
  • App Settings — Your preferences and customizations

Letters to the Ether

Feature No.05 in the Quiet Room introduces two spaces for reflective writing. Each handles your data differently:

The Letters Desk

A private writing space where you compose postcards for yourself. You can keep them, release them to the ether, or burn them as a cathartic release.

All postcards are stored locally on your device only. Nothing is transmitted. Burned postcards are permanently deleted — only a count is kept for your personal statistics.

The Shared Cove

A quiet space to exchange handwritten-style letters with one close friend. You create a private cove by sharing a QR code, and letters drift between you like messages in bottles.

This is the only feature in Reverie Mood that sends data beyond your device. All data travels exclusively through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) — we operate no servers of our own.

End-to-End Encrypted

  • Letter content — encrypted via CloudKit's encryptedValues; only you and your friend can read them
  • Whisper replies — short inline replies, also end-to-end encrypted

Shared but Not Encrypted

The following metadata is stored in iCloud to enable the feature but is not end-to-end encrypted (Apple may access it under their privacy policy):

  • Display names & emoji avatars — chosen by you, not your real name or Apple ID
  • Letter categories & delivery mode — e.g. "freeform" or "drift delivery"
  • Empathy reactions — e.g. "sending warmth" (5 preset types, no free text)
  • Timestamps — when letters were sent, delivered, and read
  • Mood snapshots — your mood shared with your friend (opt-in, off by default)

Your Controls

  • Mood sharing — opt-in, disabled by default. Toggle per cove.
  • Read receipts — can be disabled per cove
  • Leave Cove — permanently deletes all shared data from iCloud for both participants
  • Block a friend — prevents reconnection and removes shared data

What We Don't Do

  • We don't operate any servers — Shared Cove uses Apple's iCloud only
  • We don't use analytics or tracking
  • We don't share data with third parties
  • We don't sell your information
  • We don't display advertisements
  • We cannot read your encrypted letters or whispers

iCloud & Backups

In-App Backup (Optional)

Reverie Mood offers an optional backup feature that saves your journal data to your private iCloud Drive. These backups are stored in your personal Apple account and are not accessible to us. You can create, restore, and delete backups at any time from within the app.

Shared Cove (CloudKit)

The Shared Cove uses Apple's CloudKit to sync letters between you and your friend. Letter content and whisper replies are end-to-end encrypted — neither Apple nor Reverie Archive can read them. This data exists only within a private shared zone in iCloud and is permanently deleted when either participant leaves the cove.

Device-Level iCloud Backup

If you have iCloud Backup enabled on your iOS device, Reverie Mood data may also be included in your device backup. This is controlled by your device settings:

Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Backups

Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Delete individual entries (including photos and doodles) from the archive view
  • Delete voice memos from entry details
  • Delete iCloud Drive backups from the backup manager
  • Delete Letters Desk postcards individually or let released postcards expire after 180 days
  • Leave a Shared Cove to permanently delete all letters, reactions, and whispers from iCloud for both participants
  • Block a friend to leave the cove and prevent reconnection
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local data

Children's Privacy

Reverie Mood is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Important Disclaimer

Reverie Mood is not a medical app. It's designed for personal wellness tracking only. If you're experiencing mental health concerns, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page. The March 2026 update adds coverage for the Letters to the Ether feature (No.05), including the Letters Desk and Shared Cove.

Third-Party Services

Reverie Mood uses Apple's native frameworks (SwiftData, AVFoundation, CloudKit) which are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. The Shared Cove feature uses Apple's CloudKit for encrypted data exchange between consenting users. No other third-party services are used.
