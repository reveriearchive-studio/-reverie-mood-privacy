Frequently asked

Where is my data stored?

Everything stays on your device by default — no accounts, no tracking. You can optionally turn on iCloud sync to keep entries in step across your Apple devices.

How do I back up or move my entries?

Enable iCloud sync in Settings so your journal travels with your Apple ID. Your entries restore automatically when you sign in on a new device.

What is Reverie+?

Reverie+ is an optional subscription that unlocks premium features. You can manage or cancel it anytime in your Apple ID subscription settings.

How does The Shared Cove work?

The Shared Cove lets you exchange letters with one trusted friend through a private space. Invite them with a QR code — no separate accounts or social profiles needed.

Is Reverie Mood a mental health service?

No. Reverie Mood is a personal journaling and well-being app, not a replacement for professional care. If you're in crisis, please reach out to a mental health professional or your local crisis line.
